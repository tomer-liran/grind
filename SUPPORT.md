# Support

## Questions and customization

Use the [issue chooser](https://github.com/tomer-liran/grind/issues/new/choose)
for setup questions, workout-catalog ideas, and self-hosting help. If
Discussions is enabled later, support traffic can move there.

## Bugs

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/tomer-liran/grind/issues/new/choose) with
reproduction steps, browser/OS details, and whether Grind is installed as a
PWA. Remove personal workout data and webhook URLs from screenshots or logs.

## Security

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose vulnerabilities in a public
issue or discussion.

## Scope

Grind is maintained as an open-source project. Support is best-effort, and no
individualized training or medical advice is provided.
