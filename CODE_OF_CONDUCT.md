# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Grind a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Respecting differing opinions, experiences, bodies, and training goals
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Presenting unsafe training advice as medical guidance
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. They may
also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for inappropriate behavior.

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through the contact options
on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential to the extent possible.
